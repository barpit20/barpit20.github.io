I am currently employed at MathWorks, where I work with the User Experience team. I received my Bachelor's degree from IIIT Delhi in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics in August 2020. My research interests are Haptics, Novel Interaction Techniques, Wearables, and User Experience.

I am a part of the Weave Lab at IIIT Delhi with Prof. Aman Parnami where I have been working on wearables in the form factor of badges. I have also previously worked with Prof. Grace Eden on user experience for AI interfaces and digital board games. In summer 2019, I worked on the JuMP project as a part of Google Summer of Code under NUMFOCUS.

Education is something I am passionate about. I have worked as a teaching assistant for three semesters, and a significant portion of my GSoC project involved creating learning resources. I was also very involved in the academic activities at IIIT Delhi as the Secretary of the Student Senate.

Apart from my academic interests, I have a passion for cooking and nutrition. I love experimenting with ingredients and like to think of myself as a wizard with vegetables. I am also big into the fantasy genre, be it books, movies, TV shows, or games.
