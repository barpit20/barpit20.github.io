About Me

I am Arpit Bhatia,
a student at IIITD.

I am a 3rd-year computer science and applied maths student. I chose to pursue this degree because it has always fascinated me how computers make things easier for us. This has been especially true for me when practising my oher passion, Design. I started with Graphic Design and Video Editing, creating branding and videos for various events and projects. Nowadays, I am trying to combine my love for computers and design. My interests include human-computer interaction and creating new media -- be it through code, visuals, words, or fabrication.
