About Me

I am Arpit Bhatia,
a student at IIITD.

I am a 3rd-year Computer Science and Applied Mathematics student. My interest in computer science developed from a fascination with how easy computers make our lives. I love how they enable us to do amazing things ranging from solving complex problems to creating beautiful works of art. Besides computer science, my coursework has also made me develop an interest in mathematics, namely numerics and probability. Design has also been a hobby of mine, with me creating branding and videos for various events and projects.

Nowadays I am trying to combine my interests through exploring the fields of HCI and Graphics. I wish to combine my love of computer science, mathematics, and design to create immersive and interactive experiences.
